## Hi there 👋

I am a doctoral candidate in Sociology at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and a research assistant at the "Millennium Nucleus for the Study of Labor Market Mismatch" (LM²C²), focusing on social stratification through network science.

### Research Focus

I investigate how micro-level interactions generate macro-level stratification patterns through network mechanisms, examining how structural positions, relational dynamics, and resource diffusion pathways create systemic inequalities. My work spans three interconnected areas: (1) emergent stratification processes in social networks, (2) resource diffusion dynamics that amplify or mitigate inequalities, and (3) mobility and segregation in labor markets from an intragenerational network perspective.

### Methods & Projects

I combine social network analysis, agent-based modeling, and computational methods to develop models analyzing resource diffusion in heterogeneous networks and emergent segregation dynamics in labor markets.
